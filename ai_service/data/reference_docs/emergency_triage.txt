CLINICAL REFERENCE: EMERGENCY RED FLAGS & CRITICAL TRIAGE PROTOCOLS
Document ID: guideline-triage-emergency-2025
Category: emergency

1. CARDIAC & CHEST PAIN EMERGENCY CRITERIA:
- Red-Flag Symptoms: Sudden onset chest pressure, tightness, squeezing, or aching center of chest lasting more than a few minutes; pain radiating to shoulder, arm, neck, jaw, back, or upper stomach.
- Accompanying Flags: Cold sweat, dizziness, lightheadedness, unexplained shortness of breath, nausea.
- Required Action: Call emergency services (911) immediately. Do not attempt home treatment or drive to hospital alone.

2. ACUTE STROKE SIGNS (BE-FAST PROTOCOL):
- Balance: Sudden loss of balance or coordination.
- Eyes: Sudden double vision or loss of vision in one or both eyes.
- Face: One side of face droops or is numb; uneven smile.
- Arms: One arm is weak or numb; drift downward when raised.
- Speech: Slurred speech, inability to speak, difficulty understanding.
- Time: Critical emergency. Call 911 immediately. Note time symptoms began.

3. SEVERE RESPIRATORY DISTRESS:
- Red-Flag Symptoms: Inability to speak in full sentences, stridor, cyanosis (blue coloration around lips or fingernails), persistent oxygen saturation < 90%, retraction of intercostal muscles.
- Action: Emergency intervention required.

4. SEVERE ALLERGIC REACTION (ANAPHYLAXIS):
- Red-Flag Symptoms: Swelling of throat, tongue, or lips; difficulty breathing or swallowing; widespread hives with dizziness or hypotension.
- Action: Administer Epinephrine auto-injector if prescribed, call 911 immediately.
